Bar lengths show counts of unique player-week report entries. Totals are not adjusted for number of players, snaps played, or exposure.

Source: NFL official weekly injury reporting data aggregated by nflverse, 2009–2025, using the supplied CSV. Concussion means an explicit “Concussion” label in a primary or secondary game/practice injury field. Other head/neck uses a broad keyword filter for head, neck, cervical, face, jaw, forehead and stinger. Entries are deduplicated by season, game type, week, team and player ID. A player listed in multiple weeks can appear multiple times. This is not a count of new injuries, a measure of injury incidence, or proof of a rule's effectiveness. Dataset reporting practices can vary by year.
